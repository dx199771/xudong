I am a third-year PhD candidate in Innovative Media Technology at the University of Surrey, advised by Dr Andrew Gilbert, Dr Femi Adeyemi-Ejeye, and Prof Wanqing Li. My research explores image and video analysis, action recognition and quality assessment, generative AI for action and motion synthesis, and multimodal learning.

Before starting my PhD, I spent 1.5 years as an Algorithm Engineer at Aibee Inc., developing and optimising deep learning models for intelligent retail and transportation, including event detection and multi-camera vehicle tracking. Since December 2024, I have also worked part-time as a Research Assistant at the University of Surrey, investigating the impact of network packet loss on user experience in VR-based video conferencing platforms.

Previously, I completed my M.Sc. in Media and Arts Technology (Distinction) at Queen Mary University of London, supervised by Prof. Ebroul Izquierdo, and my B.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science at the University of Sheffield.
